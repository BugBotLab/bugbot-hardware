# bugbot-hardware

Everything physical about BugBot: the schematics, the bill of materials, 3D models of the robots and their
boards, the mechanical design, the assembly guide, and the circuit simulation bench that doubles as the
schematics' test suite.

Part of the [BugBot open-source plan](https://github.com/BugBotLab).

## What is public and what is not

The **schematics are public**: every sheet as KiCad source and as PDF, the symbol library, the netlists, and
the BOM with part numbers. Anyone can read, simulate and repair every circuit.

The **PCB layout is not published**: no `.kicad_pcb`, no gerbers, no pick-and-place files. The 3D models here
were exported without copper, so they show the boards and their parts but not the routing.

## Two robots

| Robot | Moves by | Boards |
|---|---|---|
| [`robots/vibration/`](robots/vibration/) | four vibration motors on a silicone mat | three 40 × 40 mm boards — Motion, Vision, Odometry — stacked on 2 × 5 sockets |
| [`robots/omni/`](robots/omni/) | three 24 mm omni wheels on sub-micro gearmotors | one Ø77 mm main board, plus a front sensor board, three wheel encoders and an NFC floor board |

They share no boards today. Each robot's own README says what it is made of.

### The vibration robot

| Board | Does |
|---|---|
| Motion | battery, USB-C charging, 3.3 V rail, four DRV8830 motor drivers, two servo outputs, the ToF module connector |
| Vision | ESP32-P4 (plus its C6 for Wi-Fi), OV5647 camera on a 22-pin flex, power switch, boot and reset buttons, LED |
| Odometry | PMW3360 optical-flow sensor and BNO055 IMU, on a 10-way cable to Motion |

## Layout

| path | what |
|---|---|
| `robots/<robot>/design/schematics/<Board>/` | KiCad schematic sheets, `BugBot.kicad_sym`, the project file, `golden_netlist.xml`, and the schematic PDF |
| `robots/<robot>/design/bom/` | the BOM with part numbers and the JLCPCB BOM CSVs |
| `robots/vibration/design/3d/` | `.step` and `.glb` of each board without copper, plus renders |
| `robots/vibration/sim/` | the circuit bench: ngspice simulation of the whole stack from the netlists, 89 static checks, scenario runs, and an interactive schematic bench. See its `README.md` |
| `robots/omni/design/lib/` | the omni robot's board generators and layout tools, including the KiCad push-and-shove fork |
| `robots/omni/mech/` | the omni robot's OpenSCAD chassis and its FreeCAD assembly scripts |
| `robots/<robot>/docs/` | assembly and bring-up |
| `viewer/` | a web page that spins the board models (uses the `.glb` files), hosted at https://www.bugbotlab.com/hardware/viewer/ |

The two robots are not organised identically, because they did not grow the same way: the vibration robot's
boards are hand-drawn in KiCad and simulated, while the omni robot's are generated by script and driven from
a FreeCAD assembly that owns the board outlines. Each `design/schematics/<Board>/` folder means the same
thing in both.

## Simulation is the schematic test suite

`robots/vibration/sim/` builds a SPICE model of the three boards from `golden_netlist.xml` and checks it
against the datasheets: rails, currents, USB limits, no-cell behaviour, motor stalls. When a schematic
changes, `python robots/vibration/sim/tools/sync_boards.py` refreshes the snapshot and
`python robots/vibration/sim/check_static.py` plus `python robots/vibration/sim/sim_stack.py` say whether it
still holds. That is why the bench lives in this repo rather than beside the firmware.

Needs KiCad 10 installed (for ngspice and kicad-cli).
